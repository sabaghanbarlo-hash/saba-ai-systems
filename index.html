Frequently Asked Questions

What do you build?

Three kinds of systems: customer assistants that answer questions and capture leads, booking flows that collect appointment requests, and lead follow-up workflows that keep conversations organized.

How does the AI customer assistant work?

It answers from information about your business, such as services, hours and policies, and collects contact details when a visitor is interested. The chat on this site uses simulated, scripted responses to show the experience.

Can the systems work with my existing website?

In most cases, yes. Assistants and booking forms can usually be added to an existing site. The right approach depends on how your site is built.

Can the booking system connect to my calendar?

Production implementations can be connected to appropriate calendar and booking tools. The booking demo here uses simulated availability and is not connected to any calendar.

Can the system send WhatsApp or SMS messages?

Production implementations can be connected to messaging providers, which typically have their own terms, costs and opt-in requirements. The follow-up demo here only previews messages and never sends anything.

How much does it cost?

It depends on which systems you need and how they are set up. Free tools can be a good starting point, but they may not cover every production need indefinitely. Request early access and we can talk through options.

Can I try a demo?

Yes. Use the Try the Demo buttons in the systems section. They are interactive demonstrations with simulated data.
